Facts on crocodiles

  • Crocodiles are cold blooded reptiles that can hunt and live on both land and water.
  • Crocodiles have been around since before the dinosaurs.
  • They will eat almost anything and they are known for eating humans.
  • Saltwater crocs are the biggest species of crocodile and they are special as they are one of the only crocodile species that can go in saltwater.

Crocodiles are ancient, powerful reptiles with incredibly strong bites, capable of replacing thousands of teeth in a lifetime, and they can even roar and communicate with subsonic sounds. They're expert ambush predators, using streamlined bodies and special throat flaps to hunt underwater, and their sex is determined by egg temperature, with warmer creating females and cooler, males. These apex predators have changed little in millions of years and possess a surprisingly advanced four-chambered heart.
