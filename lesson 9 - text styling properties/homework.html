Digestive System

The digestive system is an organ system, consisting of organs such as:
  1. mouth
  2. oesophagus
  3. stomach
  4. liver
  5. pancreas
  6. small intestine
  7. large intestine
  8. rectum
  9. anus
The role of the digestive system is to break down large, insoluble food molecules into small, soluble food molecules.
This is role through both mechanical and chemical digestion.
The body needs to absorb essential nutrients:
  • carbohydrates
  • protein
  • lipids
  • vitamins and minerals
  • fibre
  • water

The digestive system uses enzymes which are biological catalysts that help the body break down food:

  • Carbohydrase
    • this enzyme breaks carbohydrates down into sugar molecules like glucose.
  • Protease
    • this enzyme breaks proteins down into amino acids.
  • Lipase
    • this enzyme breaks lipids down into fatty acids and glycerol.
